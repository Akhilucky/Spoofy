Early preview of Spoofy for Apple silicon Macs. Includes map selection, search, popular cities, saved and recent locations, walking routes, GPX import/export, and step movement. iPhone location simulation uses pymobiledevice3 developer services.

The app is unsigned and not notarized. Compatibility and route behavior have not been fully tested on physical iPhones. Developer Mode and a trusted iPhone connection are required. Use Restore real location when finished.
